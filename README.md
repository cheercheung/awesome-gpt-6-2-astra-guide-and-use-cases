## 🍌 Introduction

A collection of GPT-6.2 Astra demos and use cases from the creator community.

**Explore real outputs across 3D scenes, animation, visual design and games.** Each case links to its original creator and pairs a practical takeaway with the published demonstration.

## 📊 Overview

10 cases · 11 demo visuals · 4 task families. All qualifying demonstrations in the reviewed collection are included; reposts of the same work are grouped together. Complete prompts are optional.

> Model labels, timings and comparison observations follow the linked creators.

### Case 1: [3D helicopter scenes](https://x.com/SadAlbert10/status/2116957078694817873) (by [@SadAlbert10](https://x.com/SadAlbert10))

**Explore procedural helicopter modeling through two contrasting Three.js scenes.**

SadAlbert10 asks for a realistic Mario-themed helicopter in Three.js without using an existing model. The paired clips show two different scene treatments: an airfield view and a forest helipad. The source labels the left result Astra 6/6.2 and the right Opus 5.5. Use the pair to compare the helicopter shape, environment and presentation against the same brief, while keeping the creator’s model labels and ordering intact.

### Case 2: [Realistic PS5 controller SVG](https://x.com/SadAlbert10/status/2116897424564515104) (by [@SadAlbert10](https://x.com/SadAlbert10))

**Use a familiar product as a test of shape, shading and material detail.**

SadAlbert10 presents a PS5 controller illustration as an SVG result, reporting a single attempt without a reference image. The public brief is short: make a realistic PS5 controller. ### Case 4: [Pelican cycling around a vertical loop](https://x.com/FiniYang/status/2116856735390683281) (by [@FiniYang](https://x.com/FiniYang))

**Combine character motion, a bicycle and a looping three-dimensional track.**

FiniYang’s first demonstration places a white pelican on a red bicycle above a coastal scene. The track rises into a vertical loop, and the sequence shows the rider upside down against an eclipsed sun before returning toward the bridge. ### Case 6: [Coastal Cruise: a 3D pelican cyclist](https://x.com/leo114119/status/2116800709006147761) (by [@leo114119](https://x.com/leo114119))

**Present a character animation inside a compact scene-player interface.**

Leo’s Coastal Cruise page shows a helmeted pelican on a green bicycle beside the sea and a lighthouse. Playback and speed controls sit below the scene, giving the result the feel of a small interactive exhibit. Leo reports roughly eleven minutes of work.
